Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Order details: name, phone number, delivery address and email (if provided) needed to fulfil your order.
  • Vehicle information: make, model, year and engine you enter to find compatible parts.
  • Browsing data: basic usage data to improve the store (e.g. pages visited).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To confirm orders and delivery details by phone.
  • To help you find the right parts for your vehicle.
  • To respond to your enquiries and messages.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

3. Payment Security

Payment details for cash on delivery are not stored. Mobile money and bank transfer details are handled securely and only used to complete your transaction.

4. Data Storage

Your order and contact data are stored securely on our systems and kept only as long as needed for accounting, warranty and customer service purposes.

5. Your Rights

You may request a copy of your personal data, correct inaccurate information, or ask us to delete your data. Contact us at any time — details on our contact page.
