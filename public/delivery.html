Delivery Information

Fast, reliable delivery — in Accra and across all 16 regions of Ghana.

Delivery Options

  • Same-day delivery (Accra): orders confirmed before 2:00 PM are delivered the same day within Greater Accra.
  • Next-day delivery: available for most Accra locations and nearby towns (Tema, Kasoa, Nsawam, Amasaman).
  • Nationwide delivery: 1–3 business days to all regional capitals — Kumasi, Takoradi, Tamale, Cape Coast, Ho, Koforidua, Sunyani, Bolgatanga, Wa and more.
  • Store pickup: free pickup at 28 Chemu Rd, Accra, Down-Right, during opening hours.

Delivery Fees

  • Within Accra: GH₵40 per order.
  • Nationwide: GH₵90 per order.
  • FREE delivery on all orders of GH₵1,500 or more.

How It Works

  • Place your order online — your price, part number and stock are confirmed at checkout.
  • We call you to confirm your order and delivery address.
  • Your parts are packed, quality-checked and dispatched.
  • Pay cash on delivery (or the payment method you chose) when your parts arrive.
  • Track your order anytime on the Track Order page.

Delivery Notes

  • Delivery times are estimates and may vary with traffic or weather.
  • Please inspect your parts at delivery — if anything is wrong, call us immediately.
  • Heavy or oversized items (e.g. body panels, engines) are quoted separately.
  • For bulk / wholesale orders, delivery scheduling is arranged with our sales team.

Questions?
