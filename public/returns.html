Return & Exchange Policy

Your confidence matters. Here's how returns and exchanges work at Plus 233 AutoMaster.

7-Day Return Window

You may return or exchange an item within 7 days of delivery if:

  • The part is unused, uninstalled and in its original packaging.
  • You have your order number or receipt.
  • The part was ordered by mistake or is not the right fit for your vehicle.

Wrong Part? We Help You Find the Right One

Bought a part that doesn't fit? Tell us your vehicle make, model, year and engine — we'll exchange it for the correct part or help you source it. Our team checks compatibility with you before you buy whenever possible.

Damaged or Faulty Parts

  • Inspect your parts at delivery. If damaged, note it on the delivery confirmation and call us immediately.
  • Faulty parts found after installation: contact us within 7 days with photos and your order number.
  • We replace genuine faulty parts at no cost, or refund you in full.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Lubricants and chemicals that have been opened.
  • Electrical parts that have been installed.
  • Parts not in resaleable condition (used, scratched, or missing packaging).

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed within 3–5 business days back to your original payment method (mobile money, bank transfer or cash). Delivery fees are not refunded except for faulty/damaged items.

How to Start a Return

  • Call or WhatsApp us with your order number.
  • Or visit our store at 28 Chemu Rd, Accra, Down-Right.
  • Bring the part in its original packaging and your receipt.
