Who we are

Ghana's Trusted Source for Genuine Auto Parts

PLUS 233 AUTOMASTER is an automotive parts and accessories company based in Accra, Ghana. From our location at 28 Chemu Rd, Accra (Down-Right), we supply genuine parts, accessories, lubricants, oils and automotive products to drivers, garages and businesses across the country.

We exist to take the guesswork out of buying car parts in Ghana. Every product we list is quality-checked, priced fairly in Ghana Cedis, and matched to your exact vehicle — before you pay a cedi.

Whether you need one spark plug or a full workshop order, our retail and wholesale service delivers the same promise: genuine quality, superior performance.
